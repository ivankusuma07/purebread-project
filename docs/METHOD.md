# Method

The standing method is published at `/method` (`app/method/page.tsx`). This is the short version for contributors.

- **Score.** Five criteria scored 0 to 10: asset 30%, traction 25%, transparency 20%, compliance 15%, durability 10%. Weighted mean × 100, rounded once. `src/scoring/veracity.ts`.
- **Bands.** 22k from 916, 18k from 750, 14k from 585, 9k from 375. Below 375 is below the hallmark: listed, not certified.
- **Order.** Highest first, ties alphabetical.
- **Deltas.** Against the previous edition, at house weights only.
- **Registration standard** (from Edition 02): a verified contract, a pairing that claims real backing, a completed launch or live market, and an interface or docs on a domain the operator controls.
- **Struck off:** 60 days without a launch, a dark interface, lost domain or keys, or a failed pairing check.
- **Nulls:** a figure nobody published is null and shows as "not published". Never zero.
- **Score policy:** see SCORE-POLICY.md.
